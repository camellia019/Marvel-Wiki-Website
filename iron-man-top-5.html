Biography

Tony Stark also known as Iron Man is the hero who started it all. A genius, billionaire, playboy, and philanthropist, Tony Stark inherited Stark Industries from his father Howard Stark and turned it into the world's leading weapons manufacturer.

Everything changed when Tony was captured by terrorists in Afghanistan and forced to build a weapon. Instead, he built a powered suit of armor with a box of scraps to escape that leads to the creation of Iron Man. After returning home, Tony upgraded the suit and publicly announced: "I am Iron Man."


Tony also became a founding member of the Avengers and one of its greatest leaders. His ultimate sacrifice in Avengers: Endgame by snapping his fingers with the Infinity Stones to defeat Thanos saved the entire universe at the cost of his own life.
