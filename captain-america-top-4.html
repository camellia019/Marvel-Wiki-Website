CAPTAIN AMERICA

Steve Rogers

Biography

Captain America, also known as by his real name Steve Rogers, is one of the founding members of the Avengers and a symbol of courage, justice, and selflessness. Born as a frail young man in Brooklyn, New York, Steve always had the heart of a hero — he just needed the body to match.


During World War II, Steve volunteered for the Super Soldier Serum experiment led by Dr. Abraham Erskine. The serum transformed him into a peak-human super soldier. Armed with his iconic vibranium shield, Steve fought against HYDRA and Red Skull.


After being frozen in ice for 70 years, Steve woke up in the modern world and rejoined the fight — eventually becoming one of the most respected leaders the Avengers ever had.
