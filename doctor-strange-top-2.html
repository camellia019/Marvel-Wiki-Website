Stephen Strange

Biography

Doctor Strange (real name Stephen Vincent Strange) is the Sorcerer Supreme of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He was once a brilliant but arrogant neurosurgeon whose career was ended by a car accident that severely damaged his hands.


Desperate to heal himself, Strange traveled to Kamar-Taj in Nepal where he met the Ancient One and discovered the world of the mystic arts. He trained hard and eventually became one of the most powerful sorcerers on Earth.


Strange wields the Eye of Agamotto (containing the Time Stone) and the Cloak of Levitation. He serves as Earth's protector against magical and dimensional threats.
