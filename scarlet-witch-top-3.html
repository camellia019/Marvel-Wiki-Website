THE SCARLET WITCH

Wanda Maximoff

Biography

Scarlet Witch, with her real name Wanda Maximoff, is one of the most powerful anti-hero in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Born in Sokovia, Wanda and her twin brother Pietro volunteered for HYDRA experiments that gave her incredible powers.

Wanda can wield chaos magic — a rare and extremely powerful form of sorcery that even the Ancient One feared.

After the loss of her brother Pietro, Vision, and her children Billy and Tommy, Wanda's grief drove her to become the Scarlet Witch — a dangerous power described in the Darkhold as capable of destroying the world.
