MOON KNIGHT

Marc Spector / Steven Grant / Jake Lockley

Biography

Moon Knight, with his real name Marc Spector, is a Marvel superhero who first appeared in the Disney+ series in 2022. Marc is a former mercenary who, after being left for dead in Egypt, was resurrected by the Egyptian moon god Khonshu and became his avatar on Earth.


Marc shares his body with Steven Grant, a mild-mannered gift shop worker living in London with a passion for Egyptian mythology, and Jake Lockley, a ruthless cab driver. All three identities are part of Marc's Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID).


As Moon Knight, Marc is granted superhuman abilities by Khonshu, which are strongest under the light of the moon. As Mr. Knight, Steven wears a white suit and uses a more tactical fighting style.
